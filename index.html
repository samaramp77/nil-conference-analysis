Data notes

What this project counts and what it leaves out.

The main modeled dataset comes from The Sideline’s NIL by Conference directory, captured September 30, 2026. Each row covers one of the 32 Division I conferences and includes its rank, tier, program count, median program estimate, combined market estimate, market share and largest listed program. Together the rows cover 354 programs.

The historical layer comes from the NCAA NIL Assist public dashboard. These 2024 and 2025 rows are de-identified disclosure snapshots. They are different from the modeled 2026 conference market. The report’s conference history uses The Sideline’s published national market history from 2021–22 through 2026–27 and backcasts earlier conference totals and medians from the 2026 conference shares. The position section uses termiNIL’s FBS football position analysis as a separate narrower model.

The qualifying panel dataset comes from sportsdataverse/cfbfastR-data. One row in football_plays_2021_2025.csv.gz is one team-attributed play in one game from one season. It has 872,023 rows, 22 columns, five seasons (2021–2025), and 332 repeating team groups. The dashboard aggregates those events to one row per team-season and one row per team-season-play-type. Rows without a game ID or team were excluded because they could not be assigned to the required time/group unit; no outcome rows were otherwise dropped.

  • Market total: adds the program-level roster-market estimates across the conference rows.
  • Market share: a conference estimate divided by the all-D-I total then multiplied by 100.
  • Power 4 share: the four Power 4 estimates divided by the all-D-I total then multiplied by 100.
  • Median: the middle listed program estimate inside a conference. It is not the average.
  • Football win percentage: wins plus one-half ties divided by games; point differential is points scored minus points allowed.
  • Historical comparison: the full-market and median conference charts use a backcast for years before 2026; they are not reported historical conference disclosures.
  • Historical change: 2025 minus 2024 divided by 2024 then multiplied by 100. The date ranges are shown because they do not match exactly.
  • Scope: values are not verified contracts, guaranteed pay, salaries, conference distributions or collective budgets. Money and winning are associated in the comparison; this project does not claim that NIL causes wins.
